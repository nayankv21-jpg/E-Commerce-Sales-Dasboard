# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard — Excel

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, product demand, customer demographics, order channels, delivery times, and customer satisfaction.

## Project Overview

This dashboard transforms raw e-commerce transaction data into an interactive visual report that helps identify sales trends, popular products, customer demographics, and key business performance indicators.

The dashboard uses **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Excel data visualization techniques** to allow users to explore the data dynamically.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and product performance
* Identify the most popular products
* Track weekly sales and quantity trends
* Understand customer purchasing patterns by gender
* Analyze different order channels
* Monitor delivery performance
* Evaluate customer satisfaction
* Create an interactive and easy-to-understand business dashboard

## Key Metrics

* **Total Transactions:** 2,400
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 11,997
* **Total Sales Amount:** ₹649K+
* **Average Customer Rating:** 3.96 / 5
* **Average Delivery Time:** 2.34 days

## Dashboard Features

### Sales Trend Analysis

* Last 13-week sales trend

### Product Analysis

* Product-wise quantity sold

### Customer Analysis

* Gender-wise purchase distribution
* Product preferences by gender

### Order Channel Analysis

Comparison of sales/order contribution across:

* App
* Instagram
* Partner App
* Target.com
* Website

### Delivery and Satisfaction

* Distribution of delivery times
* Customer satisfaction rating analysis
* Average delivery time and rating

## Key Insights

* **T-Shirts** recorded the highest quantity sold among the products.
* **Jeans** were another major contributor to total quantity sold.
* The **App** generated the largest share of orders among the available order channels.
* **Female customers** accounted for the largest share of purchases by gender.
* The dashboard provides a clear view of weekly fluctuations in sales and quantity.
